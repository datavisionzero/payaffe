# Product Vision

`payaffe` is an open-source, self-hostable application for accepting low-volume cryptocurrency payments for one operator or shop.

The product prioritizes low operating cost, simple deployment, and practical administration over high throughput, full trustlessness, or full blockchain custody infrastructure.

## Initial Scope

The MVP supports accepting payments in:

- BTC
- LTC
- native ETH

Monero is not part of the MVP.

## Operating Profile

The expected workload is small:

- 5 to 20 payments per day
- roughly 10 to 20 EUR per transaction
- no requirement to handle hundreds of payments per minute

The system should be appropriate for self-hosted deployments where storage and operational complexity must stay low.
